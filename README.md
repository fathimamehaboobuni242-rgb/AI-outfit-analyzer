# AI Outfit Analyzer

This is a prototype for our idea of making it easier for people to find and create outfits that suit them.

## The idea

Sometimes we have a reference picture of a dress we like, but we don't know what fabric to use, what materials are needed, or where to find someone who can make it.

At the same time, there are many talented tailors and designers, including people who work from home, who don't get enough visibility or opportunities to reach customers.

Our idea is to bring both sides together in one platform.

## What we want to build

The app will allow a user to upload an outfit image or enter an outfit idea. The system can then help with things like:

- Understanding the outfit
- Suggesting suitable fabrics and materials
- Giving colour and styling suggestions
- Considering the user's measurements
- Considering the user's budget
- Finding tailors and designers
- Showing ratings and reviews
- Connecting the user with the selected tailor or designer

Tailors and designers can also have their own profiles and showcase their work.

## AI part

AI will be used to analyse the uploaded outfit image and provide suggestions based on the outfit, such as its style, colours and possible fabrics.

We also plan to add personalised recommendations based on the user's preferences.

## Technologies

We are planning to use:

- Python
- Machine Learning / AI
- Computer Vision
- Streamlit
- GitHub

## Current status

This project is currently in the prototype stage and is being developed further
